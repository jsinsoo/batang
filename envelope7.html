[Suno Style Prompt]
Epic Korean musical-theatre anthem, majestic revolutionary chorus atmosphere, original uplifting march melody, thunderous military bass drums and timpani opening, bold brass fanfares, sweeping strings, full mixed choir, powerful female soprano lead and resonant male baritone responses, cinematic orchestration, 4/4 marching pulse, dramatic crescendo, triumphant finale, clear Korean diction, 105 BPM.
